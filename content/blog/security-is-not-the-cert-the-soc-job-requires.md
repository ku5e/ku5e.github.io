CompTIA Security+ has a domain called "Security Operations." It is the largest domain on the exam at 28%. CompTIA CySA+ has a domain called the same thing, at 33%. The Security+ version covers asset management, vulnerability management, identity controls, and incident response. The CySA+ version names specific tools in its exam objectives: Wireshark for traffic analysis, SIEM platforms for detection and correlation, VirusTotal for threat investigation. Security+ covers enough to recognize those concepts in a multiple-choice question. CySA+ covers enough to use them in an investigation.

Job postings for entry-level SOC Analyst roles list Security+. Not CySA+. Security+ is the string that applicant tracking systems are configured to find. The problem surfaces after the resume clears the filter, during the technical screen, when the interviewer asks you to walk through a SIEM alert and describe what you would contain first.

Security+ does not test that.

The exam objectives for both certs are public. Reading them side by side takes twenty minutes. Security+'s incident response coverage lives inside the Security Operations domain as one of several topic areas. CySA+'s Incident Response Management domain is 20% of the exam on its own, covering detection, analysis, containment, eradication, and recovery as separate competency areas. Vulnerability management follows the same pattern: Security+ covers the concept. CySA+ covers asset discovery, internal versus external scanning, agent versus agentless methods, and CVSS scoring in context.

This is not a gap CompTIA is unaware of. They publish both certs. They know what each one certifies and what it does not. A candidate who earns Security+, targets a SOC role because job postings list it as a requirement, and fails the technical interview because the interview tests CySA+ skills is now a candidate for a second cert purchase. The positioning benefits from the confusion.

Three separate comments on a LinkedIn post about the Security+-to-job gap named CySA+ independently within 24 hours of the post going up. None of them were citing the same source. All of them were describing the same professional experience from different angles.

Pull the CySA+ exam objectives before you register for Security+. Find the skills listed under Security Operations and Incident Response Management. If those match what the roles you want will ask you to do, that is your target cert. Security+ may still be worth completing for ATS purposes. Study for the role, not the filter.

The optimistic version of artificial general intelligence is not hard to imagine. A world where disease gets solved faster, energy is abundant, and human labor shifts toward things humans actually want to do. Gene Roddenberry built a franchise on that premise. The technology trajectory Altman describes follows a logical endpoint: compounding capability with no ceiling in sight.

The destination is fine. The transition is where the damage happens.

## The Operator in the Room

Altman's role at OpenAI is operational: he decides when to ship. In 2022, OpenAI's leadership was debating whether to release ChatGPT at all, arguing for a more powerful version. Altman pushed to go. ChatGPT launched in November 2022 and reached 800 million weekly users. The timing was his call.

That instinct to move into open space showed up again in early 2026. Anthropic, OpenAI's primary safety-focused competitor, found itself in a conflict with the U.S. government over conditions on its AI models. The Pentagon designated Anthropic a supply chain risk. President Trump directed federal agencies to stop using Anthropic products. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth threatened to invoke the Defense Production Act to compel compliance. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei rejected the pressure and said the company would challenge the designation in court.

OpenAI signed a new defense contract. Altman appeared at the White House alongside Trump, Oracle cofounder Larry Ellison, and SoftBank's Masayoshi Son to announce Project Stargate: a $500 billion commitment to AI infrastructure in the United States.

The void opened. Altman filled it.

## What It Cost Anthropic

Anthropic paid the full cost of the stand. The company that built its reputation on principled AI development quietly dropped its flagship safety pledge, abandoning a prior commitment to not train models without guaranteed risk mitigations in place. The stand cost them the government relationship. The pledge did not survive it.

Operationally, the company experienced widespread outages across Claude.ai and related services. Customers reported degraded model performance, tightened rate limits, and poor communication. Internal concern among employees grew.

And then Claude briefly overtook ChatGPT as the top free app in the U.S. App Store.

The user demand surge arrived alongside the outages, the degraded performance, and the dropped safety pledge. All of it happened at once. A customer base that showed up faster than the infrastructure could handle is not a sign of stability.

## The Attack Surface Problem

None of this changes what is coming for cybersecurity.

Better AI means better attackers. Phishing emails generated by large language models no longer carry the grammar errors and awkward phrasing that made them easy to filter. Vulnerability research that took senior engineers days can be accelerated significantly with AI assistance. Social engineering at scale, automated reconnaissance, faster exploit development: the offensive side of the equation improves with every model release.

The defensive side does not automatically keep pace. Security teams adopt new tools on procurement cycles. Enterprises run legacy systems that do not update. The humans analyzing alerts still need to sleep.

The transition period between now and whatever AGI actually turns out to be is a period where the attack surface expands faster than the defense. That gap is measurable, it is current, and it is where most of the interesting security work is being done right now.

Hyperfocal Distance

Hyperfocal Distance
—
meters
H = f² / (N × c) + f  ·  Focus beyond H/2 = ∞ depth of field
f=focal length  N=f-number  c=circle of confusion

Field of View

Horizontal × Vertical
—
degrees
FOV = 2 × arctan(sensor / (2 × f))  ·  Diagonal = √(W²+H²)

Star Trail Length

Trail Length
—
degrees
Trail° = (t / 3600) × 15 × cos(δ)  ·  Stars move 15°/hr at equator
Trail px = (Trail° / FOV°) × image width

ND Filter Stops

Filtered Exposure
—
seconds
Filtered = Base × 2stops  ·  Stops = log₂(Filtered / Base)
